# ADR 006: Exact occurrence identity before broader deduplication

Status: accepted for the local reference.

## Decision

v0.1 counts exact reviewed event-coordinate matches; edited-media/fuzzy clusters are a later separately tested gate.

## Alternative and reason

A similarity threshold alone can merge separate repeated appearances or preserve near-identical duplicates.

## Consequences

Broader counts require piecewise mappings and cluster versioning; unresolved alignments are excluded.

Revisit only through a documented change with regression evidence; do not silently expand the reference implementation’s claims.
