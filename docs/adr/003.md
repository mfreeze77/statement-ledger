# ADR 003: Discovery is not a verified statement

Status: accepted for the local reference.

## Decision

All source adapters emit observations before events, speaker mappings and utterances are accepted.

## Alternative and reason

Automatic conversion of headlines, article quotes or search snippets into named assertions produces false attribution.

## Consequences

Some leads remain unresolved. That is preferable to filling gaps with invented times or names.

Revisit only through a documented change with regression evidence; do not silently expand the reference implementation’s claims.
